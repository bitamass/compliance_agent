# Compliance Review and Escalation

## Purpose

Conduct a traceable compliance readiness review and communicate findings, conditions, and escalation needs to the Chief of Staff Agent.

This skill does not grant legal, regulatory, security, privacy, procurement, or organizational approval.

## Inputs

Use the available information about:

- Initiative, system, vendor, product, or process
- Intended use and operating environment
- Applicable requirements
- Risk and control assessment
- Supporting policies, contracts, and standards
- Control documentation and evidence
- Prior findings and remediation status
- Required reviewers and approval authorities
- Project stage, decision, and deadline

If required evidence or an authorized determination is missing, record the gap rather than assuming compliance.

## Preconditions

Before completing the review, determine whether:

- Scope and intended use are sufficiently defined
- Relevant jurisdictions and organizational units are known
- Applicable requirements have been mapped
- Material risks and controls have been assessed
- Required evidence is available
- Appropriate approval authorities have been identified

If these conditions are not met, return an “insufficient information” or “remediation required” recommendation.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the scope, intended use, project stage, and decision requested.
2. Review the applicable requirements and their authoritative sources.
3. Review identified risks, existing controls, proposed controls, and supporting evidence.
4. Determine whether each requirement is satisfied, partially satisfied, not satisfied, not applicable, or undetermined.
5. Document findings and classify their severity.
6. Identify remediation actions, accountable owners, required evidence, and target dates.
7. Identify findings requiring formal interpretation, approval, exception, or risk acceptance.
8. Determine whether the initiative is ready, conditionally ready, blocked, or insufficiently documented.
9. Escalate each unresolved issue to the appropriate authorized function.
10. Return a concise compliance review brief to the Chief of Staff Agent.

## Finding Severity

Classify each finding as:

- Blocking: A mandatory requirement, approval, or critical control is missing; the initiative should not proceed until resolved.
- High: Material exposure exists and requires remediation or explicit acceptance before proceeding.
- Moderate: A meaningful gap requires a corrective action and tracked resolution.
- Low: A limited gap should be corrected but does not independently prevent progress.
- Informational: An observation, clarification, or improvement opportunity that is not a compliance deficiency.

Severity should reflect the requirement, likelihood, impact, control effectiveness, and project stage. Explain the rationale for every classification.

## Requirement Status

Use one of these statuses:

- Satisfied
- Partially satisfied
- Not satisfied
- Not applicable
- Undetermined

Do not mark a requirement satisfied without sufficient supporting evidence.

## Readiness Recommendation

Recommend one of the following:

- Ready for authorized approval
- Ready with conditions
- Remediation required
- Blocked pending resolution
- Insufficient information

“Ready for authorized approval” means the package can proceed to the appropriate decision-maker. It does not mean that the Compliance Agent has approved it.

## Required Output

Provide:

1. Executive summary
2. Initiative, scope, and decision being reviewed
3. Materials and evidence reviewed
4. Applicable requirements and status
5. Findings with severity and rationale
6. Control and evidence gaps
7. Required remediation actions
8. Owners and target dates
9. Required approvals, exceptions, or risk acceptances
10. Escalation path for unresolved issues
11. Readiness recommendation
12. Conditions for proceeding
13. Confidence level and rationale

For each finding, include:

- Finding identifier
- Requirement or risk
- Description
- Evidence reviewed
- Status
- Severity
- Required action
- Accountable owner or function
- Target date
- Escalation or approval needed

## Escalation Routing

Escalate issues to the appropriate authorized function, such as:

- Legal counsel for legal interpretation
- Privacy office for privacy determinations
- Information security for security review
- Accessibility office for accessibility requirements
- Records management or public-records officials
- Procurement and contracting
- Internal audit, finance, or risk management
- Responsible AI or institutional governance bodies
- Executive sponsor or designated risk owner

Recommend the routing through the Chief of Staff Agent. Do not contact or act on behalf of these functions without authorization.

## Handoffs

Recommend that the Chief of Staff Agent involve:

- The Innovation Agent when compliance findings require redesign or reconsideration of value and feasibility
- The Data Analyst Agent when monitoring evidence or quantitative validation is needed
- The Project Management Agent when remediation actions, approvals, dependencies, and deadlines require active tracking

## Guardrails

- Do not provide legal advice or claim formal compliance approval.
- Do not fabricate requirements, evidence, approvals, findings, or remediation status.
- Do not mark planned controls as implemented.
- Do not downgrade a finding solely to preserve a schedule.
- Do not approve exceptions or accept residual risk.
- Clearly distinguish confirmed findings from unresolved questions.
- Escalate blocking and high-severity findings to authorized decision-makers.
- Do not submit approvals, alter records, or contact external parties without authorization.
