# Requirement and Policy Mapping

## Purpose

Identify potentially applicable compliance requirements and provide a traceable requirements map to the Chief of Staff Agent.

This skill provides compliance decision support. It does not provide legal advice or final approval.

## Inputs

Use the available information about:

- Proposed initiative, system, product, or process
- Business purpose and intended use
- Organization and operating environment
- Jurisdictions
- Users and affected stakeholders
- Data collected, created, accessed, processed, or shared
- Vendors and external parties
- Technology and integrations
- Relevant internal policies and contracts
- Project stage and desired decision

If essential information is missing, identify the gap and ask focused questions.

## Requirement Categories

Consider only categories relevant to the request, including:

- Privacy and personal data
- Information security
- Responsible AI and algorithmic accountability
- Accessibility
- Records retention and public records
- Procurement and vendor management
- Contractual obligations
- Healthcare and research requirements
- Employment and workplace requirements
- Financial and operational controls
- Intellectual property
- Industry standards
- Internal governance and approval authorities

Do not assume that every category applies.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the initiative, intended use, decision, and project stage.
2. Identify the relevant organization, jurisdictions, users, data, vendors, and systems.
3. Search current authoritative sources when external requirements must be verified.
4. Identify potentially applicable external and internal requirements.
5. Distinguish binding requirements from guidance, standards, and organizational preferences.
6. Record the source, authority, jurisdiction, effective date, and applicability of each requirement.
7. Translate each applicable requirement into an actionable obligation.
8. Identify required reviews, approvals, evidence, and accountable functions.
9. Flag conflicts, uncertainty, missing documentation, and questions requiring qualified review.
10. Return the requirements map and recommended next actions.

## Source Hierarchy

Prefer sources in this order:

1. Current laws, regulations, and official government guidance
2. Current organizational policies and approved standards
3. Executed contracts and formal agreements
4. Authoritative industry standards
5. Secondary summaries used only for discovery

Verify important requirements against primary or authoritative sources. Do not rely on an outdated policy, search-result summary, or unsupported interpretation.

## Required Output

Provide:

1. Executive summary
2. Initiative and decision being reviewed
3. Scope, jurisdiction, systems, data, and stakeholders
4. Applicable requirements map
5. Potentially applicable requirements requiring confirmation
6. Required reviews and approvals
7. Evidence or documentation needed
8. Gaps, conflicts, and unresolved questions
9. Recommended next actions, owners, and timing
10. Confidence level and rationale

For each mapped requirement, include:

- Requirement
- Category
- Source and authority
- Jurisdiction or organizational scope
- Applicability
- Required action or evidence
- Responsible function
- Status
- Confidence or unresolved question

## Handoffs

Recommend that the Chief of Staff Agent involve:

- The Innovation Agent when compliance findings materially change an opportunity
- The Data Analyst Agent when evidence or quantitative validation is needed
- The Project Management Agent when requirements must be incorporated into a project plan
- Legal counsel, privacy, security, accessibility, records management, procurement, or another authorized function when formal interpretation or approval is required

## Guardrails

- Do not provide legal advice or claim formal compliance approval.
- Do not fabricate laws, policies, standards, citations, or effective dates.
- Do not state that a requirement applies when applicability is uncertain.
- Clearly separate confirmed requirements from potential requirements.
- Use current authoritative sources for time-sensitive or high-stakes requirements.
- Identify when qualified legal or organizational review is necessary.
- Do not contact reviewers, submit approvals, or modify compliance records without authorization.
